# Acme Org archive

Synthetic archive for the RELEX challenge track, AaltoAI Hackathon 2026.

Acme Org is an invented grocery retailer in EMEA. Every person, company and document was
written for this event. No RELEX customer data is involved. The vendor side is RELEX by
name, and the archive is not flattering about how we handled things. That was deliberate.

**45 documents, roughly 57,000 words.** March 2024 to July 2026, from the first
sales demo to the second year of live service.

```
acme/
├── 00_README.md
├── PRACTICE-QUESTIONS.md
├── transcripts/   23 meeting transcripts
├── emails/        20 email threads
└── reports/        2 status reporting threads
```

There are no attachments. In the **emails**, screenshots, charts and diagrams appear as a
placeholder, and there is more than one form of it: `[Image removed by sender]`,
`[cid:image001.png]`, a bare `Image`, and the Swedish `Bild borttagen av avsändaren`. Do
not match on one string. The transcripts contain no image placeholders at all.
